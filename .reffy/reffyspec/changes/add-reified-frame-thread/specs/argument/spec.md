## ADDED Requirements
### Requirement: Enforcement places the break; it does not prevent it
Chapter 5 §I SHALL add, after the latency paragraph, a paragraph of no more than one paragraph stating that when a representation and its domain diverge, the break is certain and happens on the representation's side, because reality is not the kind of thing that breaks. It SHALL say that enforcement does not prevent the break but chooses where and when it surfaces: diffusely and late, downstream of the stale entry, or at one loud, named location. It MAY use the crumple-zone image once. It SHALL NOT use the slogan "parse, don't validate".

#### Scenario: Reader finishes the thesis section
- **WHEN** a reader reaches the end of Chapter 5 §I
- **THEN** the two adverbs and latency are restated as a single design choice about where inevitable breakage is concentrated
- **AND** no new technical vocabulary is introduced beyond the crumple zone

### Requirement: Hyrum's Law qualifies the silence of an entry
Chapter 5 §III SHALL add, after the paragraph on maintenance allocation, a paragraph of no more than one paragraph stating Hyrum's Law: with enough consumers of an interface, every observable behavior is depended on by somebody, whatever the contract says. It SHALL draw the consequence that the as-built load surface exceeds the as-designed one, so an entry's silence at the gates is evidence that it is a partition, not proof. It SHALL point forward to Chapter 10 for what the gates do not see. It SHALL cite the pinned source.

#### Scenario: Reader reaches "The Signal Is the Map"
- **WHEN** a reader reads that silent entries reveal themselves as partitions
- **THEN** the claim is qualified by Hyrum's Law in the same section
- **AND** the paper-contractor figure (drawings describe the building as designed, the wall belongs to the building as built) is reinforced rather than contradicted

### Requirement: Solvency is named as the one inherited loop
Chapter 6 §V SHALL add, after "a line item is negotiable in a way that extinction is not", a paragraph of no more than one paragraph stating that organizations do inherit one loop, selection by solvency. It SHALL characterize that loop as validating the frame as a whole, running at the speed of liquidation, and naming no entry: observable, never attributable, and fatally late. It SHALL state that the built loops exist to bring the verdict forward from the balance sheet to the gate. It SHALL NOT cite Simon or introduce satisficing.

#### Scenario: Reader reaches "Same Law, Opposite Side"
- **WHEN** a reader reads why organizations must build their loops
- **THEN** the asymmetry with nature is kept
- **AND** the market is named as enforcement at the level of the organism rather than the entry
- **AND** Chapter 5's two adverbs are used to say what the market's verdict lacks
