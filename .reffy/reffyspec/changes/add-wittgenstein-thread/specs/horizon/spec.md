## ADDED Requirements
### Requirement: Admitted entries are standards that can lose their point
Chapter 10 §III SHALL ground the claim that an admitted entry is not wrong but can age in the later Wittgenstein. An entry works as a standard that conduct is measured against, as the standard metre is (PI §50). A standard is not falsified by a changed world; it loses its point when the regularities it presupposes stop holding (PI §142). The passage SHALL make clear that admission fixes what an entry means, not that review is always right about whether the entry still has a point, and that the outer loop of §II checks that point.

#### Scenario: Reader reaches "Correlated Loss"
- **WHEN** a reader reads why an entry "is not wrong the way a fact can be wrong"
- **THEN** the claim rests on entries being standards, not on a definition that makes review infallible
- **AND** it is consistent with §II's outer loop verifying the assembly itself

### Requirement: The formalization boundary is set against saying and showing
Chapter 10 §I SHALL add, to the tacit (presentational) category, no more than two sentences placing the boundary against the Tractarian line between what can be said and what can only be shown, and against Langer's answer to it (presentational symbolism). Langer SHALL keep the emphasis. The sentences SHALL NOT use saying/showing as an analogy for model interpretability.

#### Scenario: Reader reaches the first category
- **WHEN** a reader reads why "The assembly should not try"
- **THEN** the line is traced from the *Tractatus* through Langer to the assembly's scoping rule

### Requirement: The relay's arrival includes Wittgenstein
Chapter 10 §V SHALL add Wittgenstein to its account of the relay of Chapter 1 arriving, in no more than two sentences, and SHALL leave Langer's closing paragraph as the last word.

#### Scenario: Reader reaches "What Is Kept"
- **WHEN** a reader reads the closing recapitulation of the relay
- **THEN** Wittgenstein's correction is named in it
- **AND** Langer still holds the final paragraph
