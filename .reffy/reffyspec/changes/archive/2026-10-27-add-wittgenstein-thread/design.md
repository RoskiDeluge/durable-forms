## Context
The source artifact offers nine suggestions, each tied to a chapter section, with draft passages and risks. It also lists ideas considered and set aside. This design records how those suggestions become manuscript edits, and the author's answers to the artifact's open questions (given inline in the artifact on 2026-09-27).

### Problem Summary
- The book's philosophical lineage runs through Plato, Aristotle, Langer, Deleuze, Shannon and Deutsch–Marletto, and leaves out the figure whose career best fits its thesis.
- Chapter 10 §III grounds "entries age, they are not wrong" in a definition that can be read as *whatever review admits is correct*. That reading conflicts with the outer loop in Chapter 10 §II.
- Chapter 9 §III says the foundation model holds "the world's average" but gives no principled account of which kinds of organizational meaning the model structurally lacks.

## Goals / Non-Goals
- Goals:
  - Make Wittgenstein a structural presence: introduced once in Chapter 1, then doing work in Chapters 5, 6, 9 and 10.
  - Fix the Chapter 10 §III tension, not just add ornament.
  - Keep every edit short. Each suggestion becomes a paragraph or two, never a new chapter.
- Non-Goals:
  - Bringing in any rig material (patchrig numbers, vindex counts, probe programmes) or anything from the Nuveris whitepaper.
  - Adjudicating Wittgenstein scholarship beyond naming the reading the book adopts.
  - Changing Chapters 2, 3, 4 or 8, or the site's structure, navigation or configuration.
  - Adding a fifth prediction to Chapter 10 §IV (see Decisions).

## Decisions
- **Decision: Wittgenstein is a thread, not a station.** (Author, open question 1.)
  - Rationale: As a single Chapter 1 station, suggestions 3 and 4 lose their footing, and suggestion 4 is the one that repairs something. All nine suggestions are in scope. Suggestions 7–9 are brief glosses that follow from the thread.
- **Decision: The Preface changes (artifact option (a)).** (Author, open question 2: "no need to be dismissive of the lineage.")
  - Rationale: ANSI/SPARC stays the primary lineage of "Logical". The dismissive clause ("closer to this book's subject than the philosophical one") is revised, and one or two sentences add Tractarian logical form as the same layer named from the other end.
- **Decision: Quote the *Investigations* in Anscombe's translation (1953).** (Author, open question 3.)
  - Rationale: The drafts already use the Anscombe wordings of §202 and §258.
- **Decision: Quote the *Tractatus* in the Pears–McGuinness translation (1961).** (Default; the author did not rule on this.)
  - Rationale: The drafts use P–M wordings, in particular 2.0271 "unalterable and subsistent", which Ogden renders differently ("the fixed, the existent"). If the author prefers Ogden, the quotations in Chapter 1 must be re-checked.
- **Decision: Assert Langer's debt to the *Tractatus* without hedging.** (Author, open question 4: the *Philosophy in a New Key* passages check out.)
  - Rationale: Chapter 1 §II states it plainly. The implementation records the exact PNK passages in the task notes so the claim can be traced.
- **Decision: Adopt the non-Kripkean reading of rule-following.**
  - Rationale: A practice gives a rule its content. It does not make whatever the practice approves correct. That is the reading Chapter 10 §II needs, and the Kripkean one would reintroduce the tension this change fixes. Cite Baker and Hacker (1984), with McDowell (1984) as an alternative. Kripke may be named only as the reading the book declines.
- **Decision: Placement.**
  - Chapter 1 gets a new, short section between §I and §II. Existing sections keep their numerals; the new section takes the next one in sequence and later ones renumber.
  - The rule-following paragraph goes at the end of Chapter 5 §IV, before the handoff to physics.
  - The binding paragraph goes in Chapter 9 §II, not Chapter 8 §IV.
- **Decision: Length budgets.** Ch1 new section ≤ 3 paragraphs. Each other insertion ≤ 2 paragraphs. The Preface addition ≤ 3 sentences. Ch7 §I and the Ch10 §I and §V glosses ≤ 2 sentences each.
- **Decision: Defer the optional fifth prediction (Ch10 §IV).**
  - Rationale: Part IV's predictions are about organizations. A prediction about models ("substrates will not induce invariants from use") rests on outside literature and on an untested rig prior. It can be revisited once `FINDINGS.md` has results.
- **Decision: Keep the set-aside list set aside.** TLP 6.43 and directives, ethics taught by example, the gate as judgement-stroke, the "three departures" frame, and PRH stay out, for the reasons recorded in the artifact.

## Risks / Trade-offs
- **Chapter 1 gets heavier.** Mitigation: the length budget above, and no picture-theory internals.
- **Wittgenstein overshadows Langer.** Mitigation: he arrives as Langer's source and as a self-correcting station in the relay. Langer keeps the last word in Chapter 10 §V.
- **The latent-space material pulls the book toward ML.** Mitigation: Chapter 9 stops at the claim and names no probes, vindexes or PRH.
- **The rule-following literature is contested.** Mitigation: quote §202 and §258, name one reading, and do not argue it.
- **Summary drift.** The Chapter 1 summary is kept in three places (`docs/index.md`, `docs/part-1.md`, and the chapter's front-matter `description`). All three are edited together.
