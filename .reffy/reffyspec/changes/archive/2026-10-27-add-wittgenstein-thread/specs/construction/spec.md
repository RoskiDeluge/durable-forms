## ADDED Requirements
### Requirement: Constraints glossed as exclusions
Chapter 7 §I SHALL add, to the definition of constraints, a gloss of no more than two sentences. The gloss SHALL say that a possibility space is held together largely by exclusions, and that exclusion was the first crack in the *Tractatus*: colour exclusion (TLP 6.3751) against the independence of elementary facts (TLP 2.061), conceded in "Some Remarks on Logical Form" (1929).

#### Scenario: Reader reads the constraints definition
- **WHEN** a reader reads how Chapter 7 defines constraints
- **THEN** it notes that constraints are what turn a list of concepts into a space, with the Tractarian exclusion problem as precedent

### Requirement: A Lasm is fenced off from a Tractarian logical space
Chapter 7 §III SHALL add a fence saying that a Lasm is not a logical space in the *Tractatus*' sense (total, fixed, the same for every speaker). It SHALL say instead that an assembly is scoped to one organization, revised by reviewed diff, made largely of exclusions, and closer to a norm or object of comparison (PI §130–131).

#### Scenario: Reader checks what a Lasm is not
- **WHEN** a reader reads the fences in "What a Lasm Is Not"
- **THEN** one fence distinguishes the assembly from a Tractarian logical space
- **AND** the definition "possibility space made explicit" is kept

### Requirement: The dense substrate is described as the record of use
Chapter 9 §III SHALL describe the foundation model as having learned meaning from the record of use (co-occurrence without correction) rather than from a practice (PI §43). It SHALL name the parts of a possibility space the record has not been shown to supply: invariants, policies, authority. It SHALL present these as what the assembly writes down. The passage SHALL be no longer than two paragraphs, SHALL NOT mention probes, vindexes, PRH or any rig result, and SHALL NOT add a prediction to Chapter 10 §IV.

#### Scenario: Reader reaches "Dense and Sparse"
- **WHEN** a reader reads the account of what the substrate holds
- **THEN** they learn why whole kinds of organizational meaning, not just its particulars, must come from the assembly
- **AND** no ML-internal apparatus is introduced

### Requirement: The binding is introduced as a downward projection
Chapter 9 §II SHALL add a single paragraph introducing the assembly's footprint in a model as a projection downward. The paragraph SHALL describe it as derived from one assembly version, specific to one model, re-measured at every swap and discarded with the model. It SHALL state that meaning trained into weights cannot be reviewed, contested or rolled back.

#### Scenario: Reader reaches "The Mirror Below"
- **WHEN** a reader reads how the waist rule applies below the assembly
- **THEN** the footprint in the model follows the same rules as projections above
- **AND** training the meaning into the substrate is identified as entanglement
