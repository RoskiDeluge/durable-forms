## ADDED Requirements
### Requirement: The mode is named as the loop's unit
Chapter 8 SHALL add a section, "§III. Resolution", after "The Loop", and SHALL renumber "The Taxes", "The Swap Test" and "The Pair" to §IV, §V and §VI. The section SHALL name the loop's unit the *mode*: an act, read against an entry, ruled on where a ruling was needed, and recorded with its address. It SHALL say, with a pointer to Chapter 1, that a mode sits in the causal order as an event and in the conceptual order as a meaning, and that the assembly keeps it as one record. It MAY say in one clause why the unit is not *qualia* (private by definition, where a mode is public by nature). It MAY acknowledge in one sentence that "mode" has a contested history in computing. It SHALL state that a mode is not an entry and not a seventh component. The section SHALL NOT exceed five paragraphs.

#### Scenario: Reader reaches "Resolution"
- **WHEN** a reader finishes the loop and starts the new section
- **THEN** the unit the loop produces has a name
- **AND** the anatomy of Chapter 7 is unchanged

### Requirement: Resolution and overlay error are proposed as measures
Chapter 8 §III SHALL distinguish the unit from the tolerance. It SHALL define the assembly's resolution as the finest distinction at which the machine's reading of conduct and the organization's ruling on it still reliably agree, with the test that splitting an entry into finer ones and watching agreement collapse shows the assembly has gone below its resolution. It SHALL define overlay error as their disagreement, measurable on every mode. It SHALL map Rayleigh's criterion (resolution = k₁ · λ / NA) onto three levers: λ as the fineness of the reading, NA as the context the reading gathers, and k₁ as the discipline of the assembly. It SHALL connect λ to the swap test: the swap changes the light, and if the pair is held, the assembly is what keeps the resolution. It SHALL present both measures as proposed, not reported.

#### Scenario: Reader asks how fine the meaning is
- **WHEN** a reader looks for a measure of the assembly besides survival under the swap
- **THEN** resolution and overlay error are defined in the book's vocabulary
- **AND** no measurement result is claimed

### Requirement: Two masks, and the question of which side is wrong
Chapter 8 §III SHALL describe two masks: the assembly as the standing mask designed in advance, and rulings made after a reading as corrections, compared to optical proximity correction, which belong in the diff queue as a revision once enough point the same way. It SHALL say that a mode should record which mask shaped it: the entry's version and any ruling that corrected it. It SHALL state that in lithography the mask is always right and in an assembly it is not, because an entry can lose its point (Chapter 10 §III), so overlay error is ambiguous by design, and that deciding which side is wrong belongs to the outer loop and to people.

#### Scenario: Reading and ruling disagree
- **WHEN** a reader considers a mode where the machine's reading and the organization's ruling disagree
- **THEN** the book does not assume the assembly is right
- **AND** it names who settles the question and where the answer is recorded

### Requirement: No implementation of the stack is named
The new text in the Preface and in Chapters 1, 8 and 10 SHALL use only the book's own vocabulary (assembly, entries, evaluations, agents, gates, rulings, the diff queue) and SHALL NOT name or describe any implementation component, service, product, endpoint or runtime. ASML and Spinoza are the only proper-noun inspirations the change introduces.

#### Scenario: Leakage audit
- **WHEN** the manuscript is searched for the names of implementation components and for the idea log's scaffolding terms
- **THEN** there are zero matches
