## ADDED Requirements
### Requirement: The mode is offered as the candidate unit of the retention era
Chapter 10 §VI SHALL add, after the paragraph on the relay's arrival and before Langer's paragraph, no more than one paragraph naming the mode as the candidate unit of the retention era, set against the bit as the unit of the transmission era. It SHALL describe the mode as the atom of kept meaning: a distinction enforced and recorded with its address. It SHALL label the claim as speculation, decline to assert it, and hand the question to the reader. Langer's paragraph SHALL remain last and unchanged.

#### Scenario: Reader reaches "What Is Kept"
- **WHEN** a reader reaches the end of the book
- **THEN** the bit of Chapter 2 and the mode of Chapter 8 are set side by side
- **AND** the claim is marked as speculation
- **AND** Langer has the last word

#### Scenario: Summary matches text
- **WHEN** the Chapter 10 summary's retention-era sentence is checked against the chapter
- **THEN** the chapter text poses the question the summary promises
