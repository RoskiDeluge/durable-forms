## MODIFIED Requirements
### Requirement: The mode is named as the loop's unit
Chapter 8 SHALL add a section, "§III. Resolution", after "The Loop", and SHALL renumber "The Taxes", "The Swap Test" and "The Pair" to §IV, §V and §VI. The section SHALL name the loop's unit the *mode*: an act, read against an entry, ruled on where a ruling was needed, and recorded with its address. It SHALL say, with a pointer to Chapter 1, that a mode sits in the causal order as an event and in the conceptual order as a meaning, and that the assembly keeps it as one record. It MAY say in one clause why the unit is not *qualia* (private by definition, where a mode is public by nature). It MAY acknowledge in one sentence that "mode" has a contested history in computing. It SHALL state that a mode is not an entry and not a seventh component. The section SHALL NOT exceed seven paragraphs, of which no more than two carry the participant.

#### Scenario: Reader reaches "Resolution"
- **WHEN** a reader finishes the loop and starts the new section
- **THEN** the unit the loop produces has a name
- **AND** the anatomy of Chapter 7 is unchanged

## ADDED Requirements
### Requirement: The monad is named as the loop's participant
Chapter 8 §III SHALL add, directly after the paragraph that names the mode and before the paragraph on unit and tolerance, no more than two paragraphs naming the loop's participant, with a pointer to Chapter 1: if the mode is the loop's unit, the monad is its participant. A participant is a purpose that takes part in the loop. The passage SHALL give, in prose, the five questions that place a participant: what happens that is worth recording, who records it, what it means, what is derived from it, and who acts on it and where. It SHALL say that answering them states the participant's complete concept, and that a participant stated this way can join without knowing any other participant. It SHALL use *participant* as the book's word. It SHALL NOT use "use case". It SHALL state that a participant is not an entry and not a seventh component. It SHALL present the five questions as a proposed form, not a demonstrated one.

#### Scenario: Reader asks who takes part in the loop
- **WHEN** a reader has met the mode and asks who reads the assembly and acts through it
- **THEN** the participant is named, with the five questions that place it
- **AND** no claim is made that the five questions have been shown to suffice

### Requirement: The assembly is the participants' kept harmony
Chapter 8 §III SHALL read the assembly as the participants' harmony: participants agree because each reads the same declared meaning, not because they call one another. It SHALL connect this to the narrow waist. It SHALL describe each participant's derived state as its mirror, the same record seen from one point of view. It SHALL give the identity of indiscernibles as a practical test: two participants that answer all five questions the same way are one participant. It SHALL correct Leibniz as the book corrects Plato. The harmony is not pre-established but kept, held by enforcement and tested at the gates, and its authority is positional and earned.

#### Scenario: Reader compares Leibniz's harmony with the assembly
- **WHEN** a reader recalls that Leibniz's harmony is guaranteed by God
- **THEN** Chapter 8 names the guarantee as Plato's flaw again
- **AND** replaces it with enforcement, in the book's own terms

### Requirement: Participants are kept apart from projections
Chapter 8 §III SHALL state that the participant, meaning the purpose and its five answers, persists, while a projection generated to serve it is derived, plural and disposable (Chapter 9). It SHALL make clear that the monad is the participant and never the projection, so that Chapter 1's hierarchy (the assembly prior to its projections) and Chapter 9's account of projections both stand. The manuscript SHALL NOT use *participant* for a projection.

#### Scenario: Reader checks the hierarchy
- **WHEN** a reader sets monads as substances against projections as disposable
- **THEN** the two are found to name different things
- **AND** Chapter 9 needs no revision

### Requirement: The monad thread names no implementation
The new text in Chapters 1, 8 and 10 SHALL use only the book's own vocabulary and SHALL NOT name or describe any implementation component, service, product, endpoint or runtime, or the applied work's term "use case". Leibniz is the only new proper-noun protagonist the change introduces.

#### Scenario: Leakage audit
- **WHEN** the manuscript is searched for implementation names and for "use case"
- **THEN** there are zero matches in the new text
